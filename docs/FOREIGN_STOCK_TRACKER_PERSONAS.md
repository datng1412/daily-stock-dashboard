# Foreign-Stock Tracker Personas

## Purpose

These personas translate the Vietnamese user research into product jobs for a future foreign-stock tracking and intelligence platform. They are not current VN SSI dashboard user contracts.

## Persona 1: New User Curious About US Stocks

Description:
Vietnamese retail user with little or no foreign-stock experience. May already follow local VN stocks, Big Tech names, ETFs, or finance content.

Main goal:
Understand whether foreign-stock investing is appropriate, allowed, understandable, and worth learning before taking any action.

Main pain points:

- Legal uncertainty and fear of doing something wrong.
- English-language research and filings.
- No clear path from curiosity to safe tracking.
- Confusion between stock price movement, FX, fees, and taxes.

Jobs to be done:

- Learn what a foreign stock or ETF is in Vietnamese.
- Check legal/residency context.
- Create a watchlist without connecting money or accounts.
- Receive plain explanations for overnight market movement.
- Save research notes and source links.

Activation path:

1. Start with "follow and learn."
2. Complete a short legal context wizard.
3. Add 3 to 5 tickers to a watchlist.
4. Enable basic price, earnings, and morning digest alerts.
5. Read explain-this-stock at the short depth level.

Must-have features:

- Legal wizard.
- Watchlist.
- Vietnamese glossary.
- Overnight digest.
- Three-depth stock explanation.
- Trust/security center.

Trust blockers:

- Any copy implying universal access to foreign investing.
- Missing legal caveats.
- Hype language or trade recommendations.
- Uncited explanations.

Monetization potential:
Low initially. Potential conversion to paid education, deeper research, digest history, and portfolio tracking after trust is built.

Success metrics:

- Onboarding completion.
- Time-to-first-watchlist.
- Alert opt-in.
- D7 retention.
- Digest open rate.

## Persona 2: Recurring Accumulator

Description:
User wants long-term exposure to a small set of foreign stocks or ETFs. They may think in monthly savings, allocation, discipline, and VND contribution plans.

Main goal:
Track progress, allocation, and FX-adjusted performance over time.

Main pain points:

- FX changes make VND returns hard to understand.
- Recurring contributions may happen outside the app.
- Dividend withholding and fees are unclear.
- Portfolio discipline is hard without reminders and allocation views.

Jobs to be done:

- Track recurring contributions manually or through imports.
- Understand USD and VND performance side by side.
- Monitor allocation drift.
- Track dividends and withholding.
- Export year-end summaries.

Activation path:

1. Choose "already have portfolio data" or "start with watchlist."
2. Add target assets and preferred currencies.
3. Enter first manual transaction or upload CSV.
4. Set recurring contribution reminders.
5. Enable dividend and FX movement alerts.

Must-have features:

- Multi-currency P/L.
- Manual and CSV import.
- Allocation view.
- Dividend tracker.
- Fee and FX summaries.
- Basic tax export.

Trust blockers:

- P/L shown only in USD.
- No clear distinction between price return and FX return.
- Missing dividend withholding records.
- No export path.

Monetization potential:
Medium. Likely to pay for tax-lot tracking, dividend cockpit, recurring reminders, and advanced export packs.

Success metrics:

- Time-to-first-portfolio.
- Number of completed transaction records.
- Alert opt-in.
- Monthly active portfolio review.
- Premium conversion after portfolio import.

## Persona 3: Existing Offshore Or International Broker User

Description:
User already has one or more foreign broker accounts, or foreign stock exposure through an offshore context, ESOP, foreign employment, or international account.

Main goal:
Consolidate data, reconcile records, and produce trustworthy reports.

Main pain points:

- Broker data is fragmented.
- Statements arrive as emails, CSV files, PDFs, and account portals.
- Cost basis, dividends, fees, and corporate actions are hard to reconcile.
- Tax and document deadlines are easy to miss.

Jobs to be done:

- Import broker statements safely.
- Reconcile holdings against transactions.
- Track dividends, withholding, fees, and FX.
- Maintain an audit trail.
- Export yearly reports for review.

Activation path:

1. Choose "already have broker account."
2. Complete legal/residency context.
3. Select read-only import, CSV, email, PDF, or manual path.
4. Review reconciliation diffs.
5. Confirm initial portfolio truth.

Must-have features:

- Read-only broker import when available.
- CSV/email/PDF/manual import.
- Inbox-to-portfolio reconciliation.
- Tax-lot engine.
- Dividend withholding tracker.
- Audit/export center.

Trust blockers:

- Broad account permissions.
- No clear read-only scope.
- Reconciliation that silently changes holdings.
- Missing deletion/data-retention controls.

Monetization potential:
High. This user has immediate willingness to pay for reliable imports, reconciliation, tax exports, and document organization.

Success metrics:

- Import success rate.
- Reconciliation accuracy.
- Time-to-first-confirmed-portfolio.
- Support tickets per import.
- Tax-season conversion.

## Persona 4: Active Multi-Market Trader

Description:
Experienced user following US, Hong Kong, Singapore, Australia, or other markets. They may monitor pre-market/after-hours activity and react to news, earnings, and events.

Main goal:
See what changed across markets quickly and avoid missing important overnight events.

Main pain points:

- Time-zone mismatch with US markets.
- Too many tickers, alerts, and event types.
- Data freshness differences across markets.
- Need to separate signal from noise.

Jobs to be done:

- Monitor watchlists across markets.
- Receive event-driven alerts.
- Read a morning digest tied to portfolio and watchlist.
- Compare market, sector, FX, and asset drivers.
- Review source-backed summaries.

Activation path:

1. Choose "already have broker account" or "already have portfolio data."
2. Import holdings or create multi-market watchlists.
3. Set market, currency, and language preferences.
4. Enable earnings, price, FX, and dividend alerts.
5. Use overnight digest as the home screen.

Must-have features:

- Multi-market watchlists.
- Strong alerts.
- Overnight digest.
- Event calendar.
- Advanced web dashboard.
- Source-backed explain-this-stock.

Trust blockers:

- Generic alerts that create noise.
- No data freshness indicator.
- Unclear quote delay policy.
- Social content without evidence.

Monetization potential:
Medium to high. May pay for advanced alerts, deeper dashboards, faster data packages where licensed, and source-rich summaries.

Success metrics:

- Alert CTR.
- Alert unsubscribe rate.
- WAU/MAU.
- Digest open rate.
- Advanced dashboard usage.

## Persona 5: High-Net-Worth, Expat, Or Multi-Account Investor

Description:
User with larger assets, multiple accounts, family accounts, ESOPs, foreign residency complexity, or accountant/advisor involvement.

Main goal:
Maintain a reliable, permissioned, exportable view across accounts and currencies.

Main pain points:

- Complex legal/residency profile.
- Multiple custodians, brokers, banks, and currencies.
- Need for audit trail and controlled access.
- Family/advisor/accountant coordination.

Jobs to be done:

- Consolidate accounts in read-only mode.
- Control access for family or professional reviewers.
- Export portfolio, tax, dividend, and fee reports.
- Monitor concentration, FX exposure, and document status.
- Preserve evidence for later review.

Activation path:

1. Choose a legal profile branch such as offshore/expat/ESOP/student/foreign-worker where relevant.
2. Create read-only connections or import statement packs.
3. Confirm account grouping and base currency.
4. Invite reviewer with scoped permissions.
5. Configure export and document reminders.

Must-have features:

- Multi-account aggregation.
- Permissions.
- Export center.
- Tax and dividend cockpit.
- Document reminders.
- Trust/security center.
- Audit trail.

Trust blockers:

- Weak security posture.
- No role-based access.
- Incomplete export controls.
- Unclear legal/tax disclaimers.

Monetization potential:
High. Potential for premium subscription, accountant/advisor collaboration, family portfolio features, and B2B/white-label partner plans.

Success metrics:

- Number of connected/imported accounts.
- Export usage.
- Reviewer invitation rate.
- Premium retention.
- Support/legal escalation quality.
