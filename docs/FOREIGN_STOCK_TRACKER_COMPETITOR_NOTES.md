# Foreign-Stock Tracker Competitor Notes

## Purpose

These notes summarize product lessons from the research report. They are not implementation claims, integration commitments, or permanent fee comparisons.

Fee, market, and account details are reference-only. They can change by country, account type, product, broker policy, exchange fee, pass-through fee, and regulation.

## Competitor Learning Table

| Platform | What To Learn | What Not To Copy | Gap For Vietnamese Users | Relevant Feature Inspiration |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Interactive Brokers | Broad market access, multi-currency depth, serious account/reporting structure. | Do not copy complex pro-first UX for novices. Do not treat execution access as the first product promise. | Legal context, Vietnamese explanations, and beginner-safe onboarding are not localized for Vietnam. | Multi-currency account concepts, activity statements, tax/document workflows, broad instrument coverage as source concepts. |
| Charles Schwab International | Trust brand, strong research, global investing support, conservative tone. | Do not copy a one-market-centric experience if users need multi-broker tracking. | Not a Vietnamese-first tracker; legal/tax/FX interpretation still falls on user. | Research presentation, trust language, support-oriented onboarding. |
| Saxo | Serious investor UX, transparent product breadth, professional dashboard depth. | Do not overfit MVP to affluent/advanced users. | Mass Vietnamese users need simpler onboarding and legal clarity before advanced dashboards. | Deep web workspace, watchlists, portfolio reporting, structured product education. |
| moomoo | Mobile-first data tools, screeners, community/content surfaces. | Do not copy execution-first or gamified surfaces without compliance controls. | Tax, legal, and Vietnamese language context are not the core product. | Rich mobile market data, screeners, event cards, educational content modules. |
| Tiger Brokers | Asia-friendly multi-market experience, AI features, IPO/event awareness, strong mobile flow. | Do not copy speculative community energy or advanced market access as default. | Vietnamese legal and tax context remains unresolved for many users. | Multi-market watchlists, event alerts, AI-assisted explanations with careful source controls. |
| eToro | Simple onboarding, social graph, theme portfolios, approachable UX. | Do not copy copy-trading or social hype mechanics. | Social investing can increase trust risk if not citation-based and moderated. | Social proof, watchlist sharing, thematic organization, but only with evidence requirements. |
| Trading212 | Simplicity, fractional-style user experience, clean mobile flows, multi-currency clarity. | Do not copy transaction-first onboarding. | Vietnamese users still need legal/funding clarity and record exports. | Simple portfolio UX, currency views, lightweight education, mobile-first activation. |
| Webull | Strong watchlists, charts, alerts, and active-user research tools. | Do not center the product on short-term trading energy. | Less suited to multi-market, multi-broker, tax/reporting, and Vietnamese explanations. | Watchlist tools, charting ergonomics, alert management, compact research cards. |
| Sharesight | Portfolio tracking, tax reporting, broker imports, performance attribution. | Do not copy a utilitarian UX without consumer-grade onboarding. | Localization, legal routing, and Vietnamese explanations are missing. | Tax lots, performance attribution, dividend tracking, broker import templates, export packs. |
| Delta | Beautiful portfolio tracker, mobile-first alerts, read-only sync, fee analytics. | Do not stop at visual tracking if cost basis, tax, and document confidence are weak. | Vietnamese legal/tax/FX context and source-backed research are not the focus. | Mobile portfolio UX, alert design, read-only sync, asset class aggregation, fee analytics. |

## Strategic Takeaways

- International brokers prove demand for access and broad market coverage, but they do not solve Vietnamese legal ambiguity.
- Portfolio trackers prove users will pay for clean data, imports, tax lots, dividends, and reporting.
- Mobile-first apps prove activation and alerts matter, especially for US market time-zone mismatch.
- Social platforms prove community can drive discovery, but Vietnam product design should require evidence, citations, moderation, and track-record context.
- The Vietnamese gap is not "another trading app." The gap is a localized trust, tracking, education, tax, FX, and explanation layer.

## Product Implications

- Start with watchlists, imports, multi-currency truth, and legal-context routing.
- Use competitor fee information only as reference-only research, never as permanent product copy.
- Build serious reporting depth over time, but keep MVP onboarding simple.
- Treat every broker/import connection as read-only unless a future licensed/partnered product explicitly changes the boundary.
- Make Vietnamese explanations and morning digest a core retention loop.
